About Stormtrace

Weather & hazard monitoring

Explore lightning, UK and European rain-rate radar, US radar reflectivity, UK flood information, recent global earthquakes, cyclone summaries, volcanic reports, tsunami bulletins and space weather. Each view shows its own source and retrieval status.

UK warning summaries work without setup; full warning details need a free Met Office key. Live floods currently cover England; Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland link to official services. Radar coverage varies and missing observations do not mean dry weather.

Pause, reconnect, the lightning window, hotspots and nearby alerts apply to lightning. Other views refresh independently while open. This app is for situational awareness; always consult official warnings.

Lightning source and history

Stormtrace listens to a community lightning feed and keeps a rolling 24-hour cache in this app. On a new install, the longer windows fill up as the app runs.

Monitoring points and distance calculations stay local. External map services receive tile requests, place searches go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and automatic location uses the system service.
